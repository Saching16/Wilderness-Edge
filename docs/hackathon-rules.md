# Build with Gemma NYC: On-Device AI for Healthcare — Rules & Criteria

**Competition:** [Kaggle — Build with Gemma NYC: On-Device AI for Healthcare](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/build-with-gemma-nyc-on-device-ai-for-healthcare)  
**Event page:** [Devpost](https://gemmanycaihealthcare.devpost.com/) · [Luma](https://luma.com/9ujmu9jp)  
**Date:** Saturday, August 1, 2026  
**Venue:** One World Trade Center, 285 Fulton St, New York, NY 10007  
**Organizer:** Bond AI (New York), prize-funded by Google’s Gemma team  
**Prize pool:** ~$2,000 cash, distributed via Kaggle (~$1,995 listed on Devpost)

---

## Overview

One-day, in-person hackathon to build healthcare tools with Google’s open **Gemma 4** models. Focus is privacy-first, on-device AI — patient data should not need to leave the device.

Beginners are welcome; no healthcare background is required.

---

## Eligibility

- Above the legal age of majority in country of residence
- **US only**

---

## Tracks

Projects compete in one of three tracks (one prize per track):

| Track | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| **Voice for Care** | Scribes, intake, triage support, reminders, accessible / multilingual patient communication |
| **Agentic Care Copilots** | Agents that automate healthcare admin (prior auth, referrals, scheduling, navigation) |
| **On-Device Private Health Tools** | Fully offline tools where patient data never touches the cloud |

Wilderness Edge is aimed at **On-Device Private Health Tools**, with Voice for Care as a secondary framing.

---

## Scope Rules (hard constraints)

**Allowed**

- Decision-support tools for:
  - Documentation
  - Navigation
  - Accessibility
  - Triage
  - Education

**Prohibited**

- Diagnosis tools
- Treatment tools (prescribing / directing clinical treatment)

**Data**

- **Synthetic or public data only** — no real patient / PHI datasets

**Model requirement**

- Submissions must make **genuine, core use of a Gemma 4 model** (prompting, RAG, fine-tuning, function-calling, and/or on-device deployment). Gemma must be central to the solution, not an add-on.

---

## Submission Requirements

Submit by the deadline on **Kaggle** (judging is on Kaggle). Each team must provide:

1. **Working live demo** — hosted web app, interactive terminal recording, or a functional Kaggle Notebook
2. **Kaggle Writeup** — architecture, how Gemma 4 was used, and challenges overcome
3. **Public code repository** — GitHub or Kaggle Notebook, documented, clearly showing the Gemma 4 implementation

**Deadline:** August 1, 2026 @ **5:00pm EDT** (Devpost); event schedule may lock submissions earlier for live demos — treat the on-site lock time as authoritative during the day.

---

## Judging Criteria (100 points)

| Criterion | Points | What judges look for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Gemma Integration** | 30 | How effectively Gemma 4 is used; whether it is core vs. add-on; quality of prompting, RAG, fine-tuning, function-calling, or on-device deployment |
| **Innovation & Impact** | 30 | Meaningful healthcare problem; creative, relevant, useful for patients or clinicians; **bonus for privacy-first / on-device value** |
| **Functionality** | 20 | Prototype actually works; live demo is convincing and reasonably complete for a one-day build |
| **Presentation & Writeup** | 20 | Clear, well-documented Kaggle Writeup; demo explains the problem, solution, and how Gemma 4 was used |
